# Arcade cover artwork

Cards use 400×400 SVG compositions with original image proportions, lazy loading and readable title fallbacks. SOURCES.json records provenance and notices. The six retained Stage 1 covers are byte-for-byte unchanged.

Basketball Stars, Basket Random, Duck Life and Duck Life 2 use publisher promotional artwork for this local identification/review build. Games load from original publisher/developer players, not redistributed GN-Math binaries. No general game or promotional-art redistribution license is asserted; confirm public-distribution artwork permissions before publishing. Publisher SDKs and advertising remain intact.

HexGL and Chrome Dino retain their MIT/BSD notices. The other retained fallback covers are original typography/vector compositions. Retired Snake, Hextris, Universal Paperclips and A Dark Room covers were removed; their historical versions remain recoverable in Stage 1.
