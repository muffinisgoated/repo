Session results

Record PASS only after real controls, gameplay, restart and sound testing. Results stay in this page until exported; they do not certify public distribution.

    Diagnostics

    One-session checklist
    1. Start the game; confirm its real title and a playable scene.
    2. Test displayed game instructions, keyboard/mouse and sound.
    3. Check movement, collision, restart and fullscreen.
    4. Record the result, close, reopen and verify cleanup.
    5. Export diagnostics at the end. Loading and title screens alone are not PASS.
